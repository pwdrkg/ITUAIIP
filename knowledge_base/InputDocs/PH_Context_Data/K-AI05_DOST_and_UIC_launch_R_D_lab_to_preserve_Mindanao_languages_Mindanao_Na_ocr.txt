DOST, UIC DAVAO LAUNCH R&D LAB TO PRESERVE
MINDANAO LANGUAGE

Published: February 18,2025 Updated: January 23, 2026

The Department of Science and Technology (DOST) and the University of the Immaculate Conception (UIC) launched an

R&D laboratory to preserve endangered languages in Mindanao on February 17, 2025.

The new laboratory, Mindanao Natural Language Processing Research and Development Laboratory (MinNa LProc) will
serve as a research hub to develop Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools and technologies, such as the Multilingual
MT System and Conversation Agent (CA), to support language preservation and technological innovation in the region.
NLP is a subfield of Artificial intelligence (Al) and computer science focused on enabling computers to understand and

generate human language.

With the extinction of Philippine languages, UIC, in partnership with the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples

(NCIP) XI, wanted to preserve the three endangered languages in the region: Manobo, Kagan, and Mansaka.

It is funded through the Institutional Development Program (IDP) of the DOST- Philippine Council for Industry, Energy,

and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD), amounting to P5 million.
