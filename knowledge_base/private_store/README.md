# Private community store

Community recordings, transcripts, documents and consent records go here during local testing only.

- This folder is git-ignored. Never commit or upload its contents.
- Ethnic origin is sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act IRR (s3(t)), and stories may hold sacred or traditional knowledge (IPRA s32, s34). Handle all of it under the consent the teller and the community gave.
- For the demo, use synthetic or role-played stories only.
